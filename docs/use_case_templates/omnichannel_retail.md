# Use Case Template - Omnichannel Retail

## Typical Questions

- which customer cohorts show retention deterioration
- where repeat-purchase propensity is weakening
- which channels drive margin-efficient growth
- what action mix should be prioritized by segment

## Typical Source Inputs

- ecommerce orders
- store orders
- customer master
- campaign and spend data
- loyalty events

## Typical Executive Outputs

- repeat revenue risk board
- channel and cohort performance views
- promotion and retention action board
- reliability and freshness scorecard for weekly leadership review

## Adaptation Notes

- standardize customer identity resolution early
- distinguish promotional uplift from structural retention behavior
- map online and offline channel hierarchies into one governed taxonomy
