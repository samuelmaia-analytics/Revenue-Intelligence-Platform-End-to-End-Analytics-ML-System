# Use Case Template - B2B SaaS Revenue

## Typical Questions

- which accounts are most likely to churn
- where expansion or recovery actions should start
- which acquisition channels create durable revenue
- how forecast confidence changes by segment

## Typical Source Inputs

- CRM accounts and opportunities
- billing or subscription events
- product usage signals
- CS touchpoints
- marketing spend by channel

## Typical Executive Outputs

- revenue at risk by segment and account tier
- opportunity board for save, expand, and nurture actions
- channel efficiency and payback scorecards
- forecast scenario deck

## Adaptation Notes

- define account grain and renewal logic early
- align product-qualified usage signals with commercial actions
- tune thresholds separately for SMB, mid-market, and enterprise books
