# Commercial Offers

## Positioning

This repository is the base for a premium analytics service line: a revenue-focused analytics operating system combining analytical foundation, executive visibility, and recurring decision support.

## Offer 1: Trusted Analytics Foundation

Best for:

- companies with fragmented spreadsheets, ad hoc SQL, or low trust in numbers
- revenue teams needing one governed system of record for recurring analysis

Scope:

- source mapping and ingestion hardening
- governed contracts and schema validation
- batch runtime, warehouse outputs, and core business KPIs
- documentation, runbook, and operational acceptance criteria

Primary outcomes:

- one reliable analytical foundation
- lower metric disputes
- faster board and leadership reporting cycles
- safer downstream dashboard and ML work

## Offer 2: Executive Revenue Dashboard

Best for:

- founders, CEOs, CROs, and revenue leaders needing a command center for decisions

Scope:

- executive scorecards
- revenue at risk and opportunity views
- segment, channel, and retention analysis
- scenario modeling and prioritized action boards
- governance and data trust visibility

Primary outcomes:

- clearer revenue risk exposure
- sharper prioritization of commercial actions
- faster executive readouts
- more credible board-level narrative

## Offer 3: Recurring Analytics Operations

Best for:

- teams that need monthly evolution, quality control, and analytical support after implementation

Scope:

- recurring KPI and anomaly review
- threshold tuning and metric evolution
- monthly reliability review
- business question backlog and analytical iteration
- release discipline for new assets and decision outputs

Primary outcomes:

- analytics does not degrade after launch
- business teams get consistent decision support
- leadership gets a managed analytics cadence instead of one-off projects

## Packaging Logic

Recommended packaging:

- `Foundation` for data reliability and operating baseline
- `Executive` for decision support and leadership visibility
- `Managed Analytics` for recurring operating model and continuous improvement

## Delivery Principles

- canonical batch runtime stays the source of truth
- dashboards and APIs consume governed outputs
- every deployment preserves lineage, evidence, and operational ownership
- client-specific adaptation is controlled through configuration, contracts, and documented implementation choices
