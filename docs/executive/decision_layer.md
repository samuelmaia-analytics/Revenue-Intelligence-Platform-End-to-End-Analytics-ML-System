# Business Decision Layer

## Purpose

Translate analytical outputs into repeatable management decisions.

## Core Decision Domains

### CEO / Founder

- growth quality versus top-line growth
- revenue concentration and segment exposure
- confidence level in the operating numbers
- where intervention creates the highest strategic leverage

### Revenue / Commercial Leadership

- revenue at risk by segment, channel, and actionability
- next best action portfolio
- channel efficiency and payback quality
- forecast sensitivity and likely upside capture

### Operations

- data freshness and operational readiness
- incident visibility and recovery path
- whether the current run is reliable enough for circulation

### Analytics / Data

- contract compliance and schema trust
- model performance and drift
- metric semantics and downstream consumption readiness

## Executive Scorecards

- revenue at risk
- opportunity portfolio
- segment performance
- channel efficiency
- forecast and scenario delta
- operational reliability
- governance and data trust
