# Executive Acceptance Checklist

## Decision Layer

- executive scorecards reflect approved KPI definitions
- revenue-at-risk view matches stakeholder expectations
- segment and channel views support leadership review cadence
- forecast and scenario logic is approved by business owners

## Trust And Reliability

- reliability report is reviewed and accepted
- insight draft is evidence-backed and suitable for circulation
- freshness and artifact validation statuses are visible and understood
- alert thresholds are aligned with operating expectations

## Operating Model

- ownership of runtime, dashboard, and exports is clear
- cadence for recurring review is agreed
- change path for metrics and thresholds is documented
- incident path and escalation expectations are understood

## Delivery Acceptance

- dashboard, API, and export surfaces are validated
- commercial and technical sponsors have reviewed the one-pagers
- handoff artifacts are complete
- next-iteration backlog is documented
